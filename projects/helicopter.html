The project

PHASE 1

GPS waypoint following

The exported plan covers configuration backup, transmitter modes and takeover, waypoint mission preparation, flight validation, and review of the recorded results.

PHASE 2

Obstacle avoidance and SLAM

The exported plan covers sensing requirements, sensor and compute selection, hardware integration, mapping and localization, simulation, and a controlled demonstration.

PHASE 3

Real-time object detection

The exported plan covers target objects and success criteria, camera and compute selection, inference integration, accuracy and latency evaluation, and publication of results.

These are planned work areas, not claims that each capability has been completed. The schedule images show the state of the saved exports.
